    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Hoover

    Our technicians utilize highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab.     Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Hoover, AL

    Thermal imaging cameras are a non-invasive tool we use to detect temperature differentials caused by leaking hot water lines under your slab. As hot water escapes, it creates a warmer spot on the surface, which our cameras can identify. This technique is invaluable for quickly narrowing down the leak area, especially in larger homes across Hoover, AL.
