    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Hoover, AL

    The inlet and outlet baffles within your septic tank are critical for proper waste separation and preventing solids from entering the drain field. If these baffles corrode or break, it can lead to clogs and system failure. BRV Plumbing can inspect your baffles using camera technology and perform targeted repairs or full replacements with durable PVC or concrete components, restoring the efficient operation of your septic system in Hoover, AL.
