    Annual Sump Pump Check-ups in Hoover

    Regular annual maintenance is crucial for the longevity and reliability of your sump pump. Our technicians perform a thorough inspection, checking the motor, impeller, float switch, and discharge lines for any signs of wear or blockage. We also test the system's operation by filling the pit with water to ensure it activates correctly and pumps water away efficiently, protecting your Hoover home from unexpected flooding.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Hoover, AL

    A power outage during a heavy storm can render your primary sump pump useless. Our maintenance service includes comprehensive testing of your battery backup sump pump system. We check the battery charge, verify the charger's functionality, and ensure the backup pump activates when the main power is interrupted. This dual protection is vital for homes in Hoover, AL where power interruptions can coincide with severe weather.

    Sump Pump Cleaning and Debris Removal in Hoover

    Over time, sump pits can accumulate dirt, gravel, and other debris, which can clog the pump's intake or jam the float switch. Our maintenance process includes carefully cleaning the sump pit and removing any obstructions. This ensures the pump can operate freely and efficiently, preventing breakdowns and extending its lifespan for your Hoover property.

    Float Switch Inspection and Adjustment in Hoover, AL

    The float switch is a critical component that tells your sump pump when to turn on and off. A stuck or improperly adjusted float switch can lead to the pump running continuously or failing to activate at all. During maintenance, we inspect the float switch for freedom of movement and proper calibration, making adjustments as needed to ensure reliable operation for your home in Hoover, AL.

    Discharge Line and Check Valve Service in Hoover

    The discharge line carries water away from your basement, and the check valve prevents pumped water from flowing back into the pit. We inspect these components for blockages, leaks, or damage. A faulty check valve can cause the pump to short-cycle, leading to premature wear. We ensure these vital parts are functioning correctly to maintain an effective water removal system for your Hoover residence.

    Emergency Sump Pump Diagnostics in Hoover, AL

    If your sump pump is acting up, making unusual noises, or not turning on, our emergency diagnostic service can quickly identify the problem.
