You should also listen for excessive pump cycling and visually inspect the tank for any signs of corrosion or leaks. We can include this check during routine well system servicing.

    BRV Plumbing offers a comprehensive warranty on both the well pressure tank itself (as per manufacturer's warranty) and our installation labor. We stand behind our work to ensure your complete satisfaction and peace of mind.

    Yes, absolutely. A failing well pressure tank often causes the well pump to short-cycle, meaning it turns on and off too frequently. This constant starting and stopping puts excessive strain on the pump's motor, leading to premature wear and potential failure of the well pump itself.

    Well pressure tank replacement involves working with pressurized water systems, electrical components, and proper sizing, which can be complex and dangerous for untrained individuals.     A well pressure tank typically lasts between 7 to 15 years, depending on water quality, usage, and maintenance. Tanks in areas with harder water or higher demand may have a shorter lifespan. Regular inspection can help identify potential issues before complete failure.
