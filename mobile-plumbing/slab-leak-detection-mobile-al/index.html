    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Mobile

    Our technicians utilize highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete. It's particularly effective for pressurized water lines, allowing us to find leaks without unnecessary excavation and minimizing disruption to your Mobile home.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Mobile, AL

    Thermal imaging cameras are a non-invasive tool we use to detect temperature differentials caused by leaking hot water lines under your slab. As hot water escapes, it creates a warmer spot on the surface, which our cameras can identify. This technique is invaluable for quickly narrowing down the leak area, especially in larger homes across Mobile, AL.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Mobile

    For elusive leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach. This advanced technology allows us to locate leaks in various pipe materials, including copper, PEX, and PVC, commonly found in Mobile homes. It helps prevent exploratory digging, saving you time and money.
