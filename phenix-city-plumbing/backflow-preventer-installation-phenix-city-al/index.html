    Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) Assembly Installation in Phenix City

    RPZ assemblies offer the highest level of backflow protection and are often required for high-hazard applications in Phenix City. These devices feature two independent check valves and a relief valve that opens to discharge water if pressure differentials indicate a backflow condition. BRV Plumbing specializes in the precise installation of RPZ units, ensuring proper sizing, placement, and testing to meet all regulatory requirements for both residential and commercial properties. We work with top brands like Watts and Febco.

    Pressure Vacuum Breaker (PVB) Installation in Phenix City, AL

    For low-hazard applications, such as lawn irrigation systems in Phenix City, AL, a pressure vacuum breaker (PVB) is a common and cost-effective backflow prevention device. PVBs protect against back-siphonage by introducing air into the system when pressure drops. Our certified plumbers expertly install PVBs, ensuring they are positioned correctly above the highest point of downstream piping, as required by code, to maintain effective protection. We ensure proper air gap and drainage.

    Double Check Valve Assembly (DCVA) Installation in Phenix City

    Double check valve assemblies (DCVAs) are suitable for protecting against back-pressure and back-siphonage in moderate-hazard situations in Phenix City, such as some fire sprinkler systems. This device consists of two independently operating check valves in series, along with test cocks. BRV Plumbing provides professional DCVA installation, carefully selecting the correct size and type for your specific application to ensure reliable, long-term performance and compliance with Alabama's plumbing codes. Brands we trust include Zurn and Apollo.
