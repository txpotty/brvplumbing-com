    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Installation in Phenix City

    The cost for garbage disposal installation in Phenix City, AL typically ranges from $200 to $500, not including the unit itself. This price can vary based on the complexity of the installation, such as needing new wiring or plumbing adjustments, and the type of disposal. We provide clear, upfront pricing before any work begins.

    Typically, replacing an existing garbage disposal with a similar unit does not require a permit in Phenix City, AL. However, if the installation involves significant new electrical wiring, major plumbing changes, or is part of a larger kitchen remodel, a permit may be necessary. We can advise you on permit requirements during our assessment.

    For light use or single-person households, a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower unit might suffice. For average families, a 3/4 horsepower unit is a good balance of power and cost. Large families or those who cook frequently might benefit from a 1 horsepower disposal to handle more food waste effectively without jamming.

    Most standard garbage disposal installations, especially replacements, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new electrical circuits or significant plumbing modifications, might take longer, up to 3 to 4 hours.

    BRV Plumbing stands behind our work with a comprehensive warranty on all garbage disposal installations. We ensure that our workmanship is of the highest quality, giving you peace of mind. Specific warranty details will be provided at the time of service.

    BRV Plumbing installs all major garbage disposal brands, including InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen, Whirlaway, and KitchenAid. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific installation requirements for each manufacturer and can help you choose the best unit for your needs.

    Common signs you need a new garbage disposal include frequent clogs, unusual grinding noises, leaks from the unit, a persistent foul odor that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. If your disposal is constantly jamming, it might be time for a replacement.
