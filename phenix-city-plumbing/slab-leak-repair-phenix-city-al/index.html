    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Phenix City, AL

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method. This involves carefully cutting a small section of your concrete slab to access and repair or replace the damaged pipe section.     Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Phenix City

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problematic area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Phenix City with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Phenix City, AL

    In some cases, especially when the leak is under a critical load-bearing wall or in a difficult-to-access area, tunneling beneath the foundation is the preferred method. This allows our plumbers to access the damaged pipe from outside your home, avoiding disruption to your interior floors and finishes. Tunneling is a non-invasive interior method that requires skilled excavation and shoring to ensure structural integrity throughout the repair process in Phenix City, AL.
