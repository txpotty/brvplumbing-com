    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Phenix City

    The cost of water heater replacement in Phenix City, AL typically ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation. Factors like unit type, capacity, brand, and labor complexity influence the final price.

    Sizing depends on the number of people in your household, your peak hot water demand, and the type of unit (tank vs. tankless). Our technicians will assess your usage patterns and recommend the appropriate capacity and recovery rate to ensure you never run out of hot water.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, strange noises like banging or rumbling from the tank, leaks around the base of the unit, or if your water heater is over 10-12 years old. Any of these symptoms warrant a professional inspection.

    Generally, if your water heater is over 10 years old, frequently breaks down, or has a major issue like a leaking tank, replacement is often more cost-effective in the long run. For newer units with minor, easily fixable issues, repair might be a viable option. Our technicians can provide an honest assessment.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater replacement involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure, and exhaust venting. Incorrect installation can lead to serious safety hazards, water damage, and code violations. It's always best to hire a licensed professional for this complex task.

    New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. BRV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.
