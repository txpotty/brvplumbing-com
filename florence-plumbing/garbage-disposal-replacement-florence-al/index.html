    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Florence

    The cost of garbage disposal replacement in Florence, AL typically ranges from $200 to $500, including the new unit and labor. This can vary based on the model chosen, its horsepower, and any necessary plumbing modifications. We provide a detailed quote upfront.

    In most cases, a simple like-for-like garbage disposal replacement in Florence does not require a permit. However, if significant plumbing modifications or electrical work beyond a simple swap are needed, a permit might be required. We will advise you if this is the case.

    Common signs include frequent clogs, loud grinding noises without processing food, persistent leaks from the unit's housing, or if the disposal simply stops turning on. If you notice these issues, it's likely time for a replacement.

    If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can often be repaired.

    To ensure longevity, avoid putting fibrous or hard items like corn husks, bones, or coffee grounds down the drain. Always run cold water before and after use, and occasionally grind citrus peels to keep it fresh. Regular cold water flushing helps maintain the unit.

    We install and replace all major brands, including InSinkErator, Moen, Waste King, KitchenAid, and Badger. Our plumbers are familiar with the installation requirements for each brand, ensuring a perfect fit.

    While DIY replacement is possible, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing connections, which can be hazardous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to leaks, electrical shorts, or damage to your sink. We recommend professional installation for safety and optimal performance.

    New garbage disposals typically come with manufacturer warranties ranging from 1 to 10 years, depending on the model and brand. BRV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services for your peace of mind.
