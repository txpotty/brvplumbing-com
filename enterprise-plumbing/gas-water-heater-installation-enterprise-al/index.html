    The main difference is the type of gas they use and the specific burners and orifices required for combustion. Natural gas is typically supplied via a utility line, while propane is stored in a tank. We install both types and ensure correct setup for your fuel source.

    We commonly install high-quality gas water heaters from reputable brands like Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith, and Navien. Our technicians can help you choose the best brand and model for your home's needs and budget.

    It's recommended to have your gas water heater professionally serviced annually. Regular maintenance can extend its lifespan, improve efficiency, and catch potential issues before they become major problems, ensuring safe operation.

    Most standard gas water heater installations can be completed within 3 to 5 hours. However, more complex jobs, such as converting to a tankless unit or significant gas line modifications, might take a full day.
