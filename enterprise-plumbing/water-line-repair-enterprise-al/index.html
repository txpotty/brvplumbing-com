    Trenchless water line repair is a method of repairing or replacing underground pipes without extensive digging. It involves creating small access points and using specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one (pipe bursting) or insert a new lining into the existing pipe (pipe lining). This method saves landscaping and reduces repair time.

    Common signs of a water line leak include an unexplained increase in your water bill, damp or soggy spots in your yard (even during dry weather), a noticeable drop in water pressure, discolored water, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use. If you notice any of these, it's wise to call a professional plumber promptly.

    Water lines can leak or break due to several factors, including aging pipes (corrosion in galvanized steel or copper), shifting soil in Alabama's climate, tree root intrusion, high water pressure, ground freezing and thawing cycles, and accidental damage during excavation. Poor initial installation can also contribute to early failure.

    While minor surface leaks might seem like a DIY project, repairing a main water line is complex and best left to licensed professionals. It involves excavation, specific pipe joining techniques, and adherence to local plumbing codes. Improper repairs can lead to bigger, more costly problems, water damage, or even health hazards.

    For water line replacements, we primarily use durable and code-compliant materials such as PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) and copper.
