    Frequently Asked Questions About Polybutylene Plumbing Replacement in Enterprise

    The cost for polybutylene plumbing replacement in Enterprise, AL varies significantly based on factors like the size of your home, the chosen replacement material (PEX is typically $4,000-$10,000, while copper can be $8,000-$15,000+), and accessibility of pipes. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a thorough home assessment.

    Common signs include frequent leaks, discolored water, low water pressure, or visible grey or blue plastic pipes often marked 'PB2110'. If your home was built between 1978 and 1995 and has these pipes, replacement is strongly advised.

    A typical full home polybutylene repipe in Enterprise, AL can take anywhere from 3 to 7 days, depending on the size and complexity of your home. We work efficiently to minimize disruption and will provide a clear timeline during your initial consultation.

    Properly installed PEX piping typically has a lifespan of 25-50 years, while copper piping can last 50-70 years or even longer. Both are significant upgrades from polybutylene and offer decades of reliable service for your Enterprise, AL home.

    While there will be some disruption, BRV Plumbing strives to minimize it. We take precautions to protect your property, cover furniture, and clean up thoroughly. We often complete the work in sections to maintain some water access during the project.

    We primarily use high-quality, trusted brands for PEX such as Uponor (formerly Wirsbo), Viega, and Zurn, known for their durability and performance. For copper, we use standard Type L or Type M copper tubing from reputable manufacturers, ensuring long-lasting reliability.

    Both PEX and copper are excellent choices. PEX is more flexible, resistant to freezing, and generally more affordable for installation. Copper is highly durable, lasts a very long time, and is resistant to UV light. We'll help you choose the best option for your Alabama home and budget.
