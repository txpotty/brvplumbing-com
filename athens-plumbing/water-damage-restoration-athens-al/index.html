    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Damage Restoration in Athens

    The cost of water damage restoration in Athens, AL varies widely depending on the extent of the damage, the size of the affected area, and the type of water (clean, gray, or black water). We provide a detailed estimate after our initial assessment.

    For minor water damage, you might be able to stay in your home. However, for extensive damage, especially involving contaminated water or significant structural drying, it may be safer and more comfortable to temporarily relocate. We will advise you based on your specific situation.

    We utilize advanced equipment, including powerful submersible pumps and industrial-grade wet/dry vacuums for water extraction, high-velocity air movers, and commercial dehumidifiers for thorough drying. We also use specialized moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras to detect hidden moisture in walls and floors.

    BRV Plumbing offers 24/7 emergency response for water damage. Our goal is to arrive at your Athens property within a few hours of your call, as prompt action is critical in mitigating damage and preventing further issues like mold growth.

    Most homeowner's insurance policies cover sudden and accidental water damage, such as from a burst pipe or appliance malfunction. However, damage from floods or neglect (like long-term leaks) may not be covered. We can help you document the damage for your insurance claim and work with your provider.

    Water damage can stem from any plumbing brand. We commonly see issues with water heaters from brands like Rheem or Bradford White, washing machines from Whirlpool or Samsung, dishwashers from Bosch or GE, and even supply lines from various faucet brands. Our focus is on repairing the damage, regardless of the brand.

    Signs of hidden water damage in your Athens home include musty odors, discoloration or staining on walls or ceilings, peeling paint, warped flooring, or an unexplained increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's wise to call for an inspection.
