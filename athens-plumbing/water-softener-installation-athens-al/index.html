    Salt-Based Water Softener Installation in Athens

    Salt-based (ion exchange) water softeners are the most common and effective solution for truly softening water. These systems use a resin bed to exchange hard minerals like calcium and magnesium with sodium ions. BRV Plumbing expertly installs various models of salt-based softeners, ensuring proper connection to your main water line, correct drainage setup, and accurate programming for regeneration cycles in your Athens home.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Athens, AL

    For homeowners in Athens, AL looking for an alternative to traditional salt-based systems, we offer salt-free water conditioner installation. These systems don't remove minerals but rather alter their crystalline structure, preventing them from adhering to surfaces. While not true softeners, they can be effective at reducing scale buildup without requiring salt or producing brine waste. We can help you determine if this is the right choice for your home.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Athens

    A whole-house water softener system is installed directly on your main water line, treating all the water entering your Athens home. This ensures every faucet, shower, and appliance receives soft water. Our installation process involves carefully integrating the system into your existing plumbing, including necessary bypass valves and pressure regulators, to provide comprehensive protection against hard water throughout your property.

    The warranty typically covers both the water softener unit itself, which varies by manufacturer (often 5-10 years on major components), and our installation workmanship, usually for one year.     We install a wide variety of reputable water softener brands, including but not limited to Culligan, Pelican, Fleck, Kenmore, Whirlpool, and GE. We can also help you choose the best brand and model to suit your specific water quality needs and budget.

    A properly sized and installed water softener should not significantly affect your water pressure. If you experience a noticeable drop in pressure after installation, it could indicate an issue with the system or installation, which our plumbers can diagnose and resolve.

    Maintenance primarily involves periodically adding salt to salt-based systems, typically every 4-8 weeks depending on water usage and hardness.
