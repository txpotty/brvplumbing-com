    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Montgomery

    The cost of garbage disposal replacement in Montgomery, AL typically ranges from $200 to $500, including the new unit and labor. This can vary based on the model chosen, its horsepower, and any necessary plumbing modifications. We provide a detailed quote upfront.

    While DIY replacement is possible, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing connections, which can be hazardous if not done correctly.     For light use, a 1/3 HP or 1/2 HP unit is usually sufficient. For regular use and tough food scraps, a 3/4 HP or 1 HP unit is recommended. Our plumbers can help you assess your needs and choose the best fit for your Montgomery home.

    In most cases, a simple like-for-like garbage disposal replacement in Montgomery does not require a permit. However, if significant plumbing modifications or electrical work beyond a simple swap are needed, a permit might be required. We will advise you if this is the case.
