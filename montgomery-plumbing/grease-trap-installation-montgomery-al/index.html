    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Montgomery

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. BRV Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Montgomery for maximum efficiency.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Montgomery, AL

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, ensuring compliance with all local Alabama ordinances for waste management.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Installation in Montgomery

    The cost of grease trap installation in Montgomery, AL varies widely based on the type and size of the trap, whether it's an indoor or outdoor unit, and the complexity of the installation. For a small under-sink hydro-mechanical unit, expect to pay between $1,500 and $4,000. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors can range from $7,000 to $20,000 or more, including excavation and concrete work.

    Yes, for most commercial grease trap installations in Montgomery, a plumbing permit from the local building department is required. This ensures the installation complies with local codes and environmental regulations. Our team can help guide you through the permitting process.

    While the terms are often used interchangeably, 'grease trap' typically refers to smaller, indoor hydro-mechanical units, often found under sinks. 'Grease interceptor' usually refers to larger, outdoor gravity-fed units with higher capacities. Both serve the same purpose of separating FOG from wastewater.

    The timeline for grease trap installation depends on the project's scope. A simple under-sink unit might take a few hours. Larger outdoor gravity interceptors, which involve excavation and more extensive plumbing, can take anywhere from one to several days. We always aim to minimize disruption to your business operations.

    BRV Plumbing provides a warranty on our workmanship for all grease trap installations. Additionally, the grease trap unit itself will typically come with a manufacturer's warranty. We will provide you with all warranty details at the time of installation, ensuring your peace of mind.

    Signs you might need a new grease trap include frequent sewer backups, persistent foul odors coming from drains, slow drainage throughout your kitchen, or if your current unit is visibly corroded or leaking. If your existing trap is old and no longer meets current local codes in Montgomery, an upgrade is also advisable.

    Grease trap installation, especially for commercial applications, is a complex process that requires specialized knowledge of plumbing codes, proper sizing, and connection techniques. Attempting a DIY installation can lead to code violations, inefficient operation, and costly plumbing issues down the line. It is always recommended to hire a licensed professional.

    The lifespan of a grease trap depends on its type, material, and how well it's maintained. Steel grease traps may last 10-15 years, while durable fiberglass or polyethylene units can last 20-30 years or even longer. Regular cleaning and proper usage are key to extending its lifespan.
