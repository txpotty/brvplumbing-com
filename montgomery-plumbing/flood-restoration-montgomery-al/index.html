    For basic water extraction and drying, permits are generally not required. However, if the flood damage necessitates major structural repairs, electrical work, or extensive plumbing overhauls, then permits from the Montgomery building department may be required. We will advise you on any necessary permits and can assist with the application process.

    Most homeowner's insurance policies cover water damage from sudden and accidental events, like a burst pipe or water heater leak. However, damage from natural floods typically requires a separate flood insurance policy. We can help you navigate the claims process and provide documentation for your insurer.

    The duration of flood restoration depends on the severity of the water damage. A typical process involving water extraction, drying, and basic repairs can take anywhere from 3 to 7 days.
