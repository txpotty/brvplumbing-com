    Yes, tankless water heaters are very energy efficient in Alabama's climate. Since they only heat water on demand, they avoid the 'standby heat loss' common with traditional tanks, which can be significant even in warmer climates, especially during milder seasons.

    Yes, gas tankless water heaters require specific venting materials and configurations different from traditional tank heaters. Many modern units use PVC or polypropylene for venting, and proper slope and termination are critical for safe and efficient operation. Electric units do not require venting.

    Tankless water heaters benefit from annual flushing to remove mineral buildup, especially in areas with hard water like parts of Alabama. This helps maintain efficiency and prolong the unit's lifespan. We offer maintenance services to keep your system running optimally.
