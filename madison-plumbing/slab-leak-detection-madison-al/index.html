    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Madison

    Our technicians utilize highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab. This method involves listening for the subtle sound of escaping water, even through concrete.     Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Madison, AL

    Thermal imaging cameras are a non-invasive tool we use to detect temperature differentials caused by leaking hot water lines under your slab. As hot water escapes, it creates a warmer spot on the surface, which our cameras can identify. This technique is invaluable for quickly narrowing down the leak area, especially in larger homes across Madison, AL.

    Electronic Leak Detection in Madison

    For elusive leaks, our electronic leak detection equipment sends a signal through your pipes and measures changes that indicate a breach.     Common signs of a slab leak in an Alabama home include unusually high water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, visible cracks in the foundation, damp or wet spots on carpets or flooring, and a sudden drop in water pressure.
