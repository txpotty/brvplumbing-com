    Tankless water heater maintenance focuses primarily on descaling the heat exchanger and checking electronic components for efficiency. Hot water tank maintenance typically involves flushing sediment from the bottom of the tank, checking the anode rod for corrosion, and inspecting the pressure relief valve.     The descaling process involves shutting off the unit, isolating the water lines, and connecting a circulating pump to flush a specialized acidic solution through the heat exchanger. This solution dissolves mineral deposits like calcium and magnesium, which are common in Alabama water, restoring optimal heat transfer efficiency.
