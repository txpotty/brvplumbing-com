    Submersible pumps are common for deeper wells, operating silently within the well casing itself.     Jet Pump Replacement in Gadsden, AL

    Jet pumps are typically used for shallower wells and are installed above ground, often in a basement or utility room. Common reasons for replacement include motor burnout, impeller wear, or a cracked casing from freezing.     The lifespan of a well pump varies greatly depending on usage, water quality, and pump type, but most last between 10 to 20 years. Regular maintenance and proper sizing can extend its life, but eventually, wear and tear will necessitate replacement.

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up from shallower wells, typically less than 25 feet. Submersible pumps are placed directly inside the well casing, submerged in water, and are used for deeper wells, pushing water up to the surface. Each has specific applications and benefits.
