    Common signs include frequent clogs, loud grinding noises without processing food, persistent leaks from the unit's housing, or if the disposal simply stops turning on. If you notice these issues, it's likely time for a replacement.

    While DIY replacement is possible, it involves working with both electricity and plumbing connections, which can be hazardous if not done correctly.     If your disposal is old, frequently leaking, or making grinding noises without grinding food, replacement is usually more cost-effective and provides a longer-lasting solution. Minor issues like a simple jam can often be repaired.
