    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in Vestavia Hills

    For significant damage or older, failing systems, traditional sewer line replacement often involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire pipe. This method is effective for replacing severely collapsed or extensively rooted lines, especially common in older Vestavia Hills homes with cast iron or clay pipes. We carefully plan the excavation to minimize disruption to your yard and landscaping, using heavy-duty equipment for efficient digging and backfilling.

    Trenchless Sewer Replacement Options in Vestavia Hills, AL

    When appropriate, trenchless sewer replacement offers a less invasive alternative to traditional digging.
