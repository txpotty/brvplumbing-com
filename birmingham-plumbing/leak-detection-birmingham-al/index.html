Early detection of a slab leak prevents serious foundation damage, mold growth, and costly structural repairs.

    Water Leak Detection in Walls & Ceilings in Birmingham, AL

    Water leaks behind walls or in ceilings can go unnoticed for long periods, leading to extensive drywall damage, wood rot, and mold.
