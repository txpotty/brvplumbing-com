    Slab Leak Detection and Repair in Birmingham

    Slab leaks are common in homes built on concrete foundations, often caused by shifting soil or corroded pipes underneath. Our technicians use non-invasive methods like electronic leak detection and hydrostatic pressure testing to find these elusive leaks. Once located, we can perform targeted repairs, either by slab tunneling or direct access, minimizing disruption to your home. We work with various pipe materials, including copper, PEX, and PVC, ensuring a durable repair for your Birmingham property.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Birmingham, AL

    Crawl space foundations in Alabama are susceptible to leaks from plumbing, groundwater, or HVAC condensation. These leaks can lead to wood rot, mold, and structural damage if not addressed quickly. Our team thoroughly inspects the crawl space, identifying the source of water intrusion. We then implement solutions such as pipe repair, sump pump installation, or vapor barrier replacement to dry out and protect your crawl space in Birmingham, AL from future moisture issues.

    Exterior Foundation Leak Solutions in Birmingham

    Many foundation leaks originate from exterior factors like poor drainage, overflowing gutters, or saturated soil. We assess your home's exterior to identify these culprits. Our solutions include grading adjustments, French drain installation, and downspout extensions to redirect water away from your foundation. Proper exterior management is crucial for preventing hydrostatic pressure buildup against your foundation walls in Birmingham and preventing water intrusion.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Birmingham, AL
