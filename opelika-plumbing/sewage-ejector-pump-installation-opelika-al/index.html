    The cost of sewage ejector pump installation in Opelika, AL typically ranges from $2,500 to $6,000, depending on factors like pump type, basin size, excavation needs, and whether new electrical circuits are required.     Regular maintenance for a sewage ejector pump involves periodically checking the pump basin for debris, ensuring the check valve is functioning correctly, and testing the float switch. It's also wise to avoid flushing non-biodegradable items. BRV Plumbing offers maintenance services to keep your pump running efficiently and prevent unexpected breakdowns.

    BRV Plumbing installs a variety of high-quality and reliable sewage ejector pump brands, including Zoeller, Liberty Pumps, Wayne, and Grundfos. We choose brands known for their durability and performance, ensuring you get a pump that will last. Our technicians can help you select the best brand and model for your specific needs and budget.

    A sewage ejector pump is specifically designed to handle solid waste and wastewater from plumbing fixtures below the main sewer line, like toilets and sinks, discharging it into the sewer system. A sump pump, in contrast, is used to remove groundwater from a basement or crawl space, only handling clear water to prevent flooding. They serve distinct purposes for different types of water.

    BRV Plumbing provides a comprehensive warranty on all our sewage ejector pump installations, covering both parts and labor.     A well-maintained sewage ejector pump typically has a lifespan of 7 to 10 years, though some higher-quality units can last longer.
