    The cost of water heater replacement in Opelika, AL typically ranges from $1,200 to $2,500 for a standard tank unit, including installation.     New water heaters typically come with a manufacturer's warranty on the tank and parts, usually ranging from 6 to 12 years. BRV Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services, giving you added peace of mind. Specific warranty details vary by brand and model.

    Sizing depends on the number of people in your household, your peak hot water demand, and the type of unit (tank vs. tankless). Our technicians will assess your usage patterns and recommend the appropriate capacity and recovery rate to ensure you never run out of hot water.

    Yes, in most areas of Alabama, a permit is required for water heater replacement to ensure the installation meets local building and plumbing codes. BRV Plumbing handles all necessary permitting processes, ensuring your installation is fully compliant and safe.

    While it might seem like a DIY project, water heater replacement involves working with gas lines, high voltage electricity, water pressure, and exhaust venting. Incorrect installation can lead to serious safety hazards, water damage, and code violations. It's always best to hire a licensed professional for this complex task.

    Upgrading provides more reliable hot water, improved energy efficiency leading to lower utility bills, better performance, and reduced risk of leaks or breakdowns. Newer models often have advanced features and longer warranties, enhancing comfort and safety in your home.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, strange noises like banging or rumbling from the tank, leaks around the base of the unit, or if your water heater is over 10-12 years old. Any of these symptoms warrant a professional inspection.
