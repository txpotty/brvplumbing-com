    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Huntsville

    Submersible sump pumps are designed to sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, making them quieter and more discreet. We specialize in replacing these units with high-efficiency models from leading brands like Zoeller and Wayne. Our technicians ensure the new submersible pump has adequate horsepower and flow rate for your home's needs, considering Alabama's potential for heavy rainfall. We also check and replace any worn check valves and discharge piping during the installation process.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Huntsville, AL

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more affordable. They are a good choice for narrower or shallower pits. BRV Plumbing provides expert replacement of pedestal sump pumps, ensuring the new unit is properly aligned and connected. We consider the typical basement structures in older Huntsville homes when recommending and installing these pumps, making sure they fit well and perform reliably.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Huntsville

    Power outages during severe storms can render a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units.     Common signs you need a new sump pump include unusual noises like grinding or rattling, visible rust or corrosion on the unit, frequent cycling on and off, or if it fails to turn on during heavy rain.     While it might seem like a simple task, sump pump replacement involves electrical connections, proper plumbing, and ensuring the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding.     A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity.
