    Water Heater Maintenance in Huntsville

    Regular water heater maintenance is crucial for efficiency and longevity, especially given the mineral content in some Alabama water supplies. We drain sediment, check anode rods for corrosion, inspect heating elements or burners, and verify pressure relief valve operation. This preventative service helps avoid unexpected cold showers and extends the life of your unit, whether it's a traditional tank or a tankless system.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Huntsville, AL

    Protecting your basement from flooding is vital in Alabama, particularly during heavy rain seasons. Our sump pump maintenance includes checking the float switch, cleaning the pit, testing the discharge line for obstructions, and ensuring the pump activates correctly. We also inspect the check valve and battery backup systems to guarantee reliable operation when you need it most, preventing costly water damage restoration.

    Water Softener Maintenance in Huntsville

    Hard water is a common issue in many Alabama homes, leading to scale buildup in pipes and appliances. Our water softener maintenance service involves checking brine tank levels, cleaning injectors, inspecting resin beds, and verifying regeneration cycles. Proper maintenance ensures your softener continues to effectively remove minerals, protecting your plumbing and extending the life of your water-using appliances.
