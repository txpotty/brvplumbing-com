    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Installation in Auburn

    The cost for garbage disposal installation in Auburn, AL typically ranges from $200 to $500, not including the unit itself. This price can vary based on the complexity of the installation, such as needing new wiring or plumbing adjustments, and the type of disposal. We provide clear, upfront pricing before any work begins.

    BRV Plumbing installs all major garbage disposal brands, including InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen, Whirlaway, and KitchenAid. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific installation requirements for each manufacturer and can help you choose the best unit for your needs.

    For light use or single-person households, a 1/3 or 1/2 horsepower unit might suffice. For average families, a 3/4 horsepower unit is a good balance of power and cost. Large families or those who cook frequently might benefit from a 1 horsepower disposal to handle more food waste effectively without jamming.

    While it's possible for a handy homeowner to install a garbage disposal, we strongly recommend professional installation. Incorrect wiring can create electrical hazards, and improper plumbing connections can lead to leaks and water damage. Our licensed plumbers ensure a safe, code-compliant, and leak-free installation.

    Most standard garbage disposal installations, especially replacements, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new electrical circuits or significant plumbing modifications, might take longer, up to 3 to 4 hours.

    Typically, replacing an existing garbage disposal with a similar unit does not require a permit in Auburn, AL. However, if the installation involves significant new electrical wiring, major plumbing changes, or is part of a larger kitchen remodel, a permit may be necessary. We can advise you on permit requirements during our assessment.

    Most standard kitchen sinks are compatible with garbage disposals, provided they have a drain opening of 3 1/2 to 4 inches. Some sinks, particularly older cast iron models or those with unique drain configurations, may require adapters or modifications. Our plumbers can assess your sink's compatibility.

    To maintain your new garbage disposal, always run cold water before, during, and after grinding food waste. Avoid putting hard items like bones, fibrous vegetables, or grease down the drain. Occasionally grind citrus peels or ice cubes to help clean the blades and freshen the unit.
