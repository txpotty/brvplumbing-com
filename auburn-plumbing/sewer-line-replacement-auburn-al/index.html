    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Auburn

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching offers a robust solution. This method involves excavating a trench to access and replace the entire damaged section of pipe. While more invasive, it allows for direct inspection of the surrounding soil and ensures a complete replacement with new, durable materials like PVC, which is excellent for resisting root intrusion common in Alabama soil. This approach is often necessary for older cast iron or clay pipes that have deteriorated beyond repair.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement in Auburn, AL

    Minimizing disruption, trenchless sewer line replacement involves replacing pipes without extensive digging. We offer pipe bursting, where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, fracturing the old pipe outwards. This is ideal for situations where maintaining landscaping or driveways is a priority. This method is highly effective for many residential properties in Auburn and can often be completed faster than traditional methods, using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipes known for their longevity.
