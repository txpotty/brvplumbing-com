    Protecting your drinking water from contamination is vital.     PEX Pipe Installation in Auburn

    PEX piping has become a popular choice for new plumbing installations and repiping projects in Auburn due to its flexibility, durability, and resistance to corrosion and freezing.     In Auburn, most backflow preventers are required to be tested annually by a certified technician to ensure they are functioning correctly. This is crucial for maintaining the safety of your public water supply. BRV Plumbing offers convenient annual testing and certification services.

    Many significant plumbing installations in Auburn require a permit from the local building department. This typically includes water heater replacements, gas line installations, new sewer lines, or any major pipe alterations. BRV Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and ensures your project complies with local codes.

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible plastic tubing used for water supply lines. It's popular for installations in Auburn because it's corrosion-resistant, less prone to bursting from freezing than copper, and often quicker to install. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings, reducing potential leak points and making it a durable choice for Alabama homes.

    BRV Plumbing installs a wide range of reputable brands, including Moen, Delta, Kohler for fixtures, Rheem, Bradford White, and Rinnai for water heaters, and Zoeller or Liberty Pumps for sump and ejector pumps.     Common signs you might need new water lines in your Auburn home include consistently low water pressure, discolored water (especially reddish-brown), frequent leaks, or very old pipes (like galvanized steel).     You likely need a sump pump if your Auburn home has a basement or crawl space that experiences water seepage, dampness, or has flooded in the past.
