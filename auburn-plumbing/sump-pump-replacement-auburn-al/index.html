    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Auburn

    Submersible sump pumps are designed to sit directly in the sump pit, submerged in water, making them quieter and more discreet. Our technicians ensure the new submersible pump has adequate horsepower and flow rate for your home's needs, considering Alabama's potential for heavy rainfall.     Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Auburn, AL

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more affordable. They are a good choice for narrower or shallower pits. BRV Plumbing provides expert replacement of pedestal sump pumps, ensuring the new unit is properly aligned and connected. We consider the typical basement structures in older Auburn homes when recommending and installing these pumps, making sure they fit well and perform reliably.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Auburn

    Power outages during severe storms can render a primary sump pump useless, leading to basement flooding. A battery backup sump pump offers crucial protection during these times. We install and replace complete battery backup systems, including dedicated marine-grade batteries and smart charging units.     Yes, in addition to replacement, we offer comprehensive sump pump maintenance services to ensure your system is always ready. Regular maintenance, including cleaning the pit, checking the float switch, and inspecting the discharge line, can extend the life of your pump and prevent unexpected failures. This is especially important for homes in Auburn that rely heavily on their sump pumps.

    A standard sump pump replacement typically takes between 2 to 4 hours. This includes removing the old unit, installing the new pump, connecting plumbing and electrical, and testing the system. More complex installations, such as those requiring pit modifications or new discharge lines, may take longer.

    Common signs you need a new sump pump include unusual noises like grinding or rattling, visible rust or corrosion on the unit, frequent cycling on and off, or if it fails to turn on during heavy rain. If your pump is over 7-10 years old, it's also a good idea to consider replacement proactively, especially in Alabama where heavy rains can be unpredictable.

    We install and replace high-quality sump pumps from reputable brands known for their reliability and performance. Our common brands include Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps, and Little Giant. We can also work with other brands based on your preference or specific requirements, ensuring you get a durable solution.

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, serves as a secondary system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power, or cannot keep up with excessive water inflow. Many Auburn homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.

    In most cases, a direct like-for-like replacement of a sump pump does not require a specific plumbing permit in Auburn, AL.
