    Slab Leak Detection in Auburn

    Slab leaks, common in homes with concrete foundations, can be particularly destructive if not addressed quickly. We utilize specialized acoustic equipment to listen for the precise sound of water escaping under your slab, allowing us to pinpoint the leak with minimal disruption. Our team also uses thermal imaging to identify temperature differentials caused by escaping water, further confirming the leak's location before any concrete is disturbed. Early detection of slab leaks helps prevent serious foundation damage, which can be very costly to repair in Alabama homes.

    Pinhole Leak Detection in Auburn, AL

    Pinhole leaks, often found in copper or PEX piping, are small but can cause significant damage over time, especially behind walls or under floors. We employ non-invasive moisture meters to detect elevated humidity levels and thermal cameras to identify cold spots indicative of water presence. Our plumbers are adept at tracing these elusive leaks without tearing apart your entire home. Swift action on pinhole leaks prevents extensive drywall damage, mold growth, and compromised structural integrity, common issues in Auburn's humid climate.
