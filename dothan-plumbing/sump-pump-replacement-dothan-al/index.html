    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Dothan, AL

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more affordable. They are a good choice for narrower or shallower pits. BRV Plumbing provides expert replacement of pedestal sump pumps, ensuring the new unit is properly aligned and connected.     A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding, powered by your home's electricity. A backup sump pump, usually battery-powered or water-powered, serves as a secondary system that activates if the primary pump fails, loses power, or cannot keep up with excessive water inflow. Many Dothan homes benefit from a backup system due to storm risks.

    A standard sump pump replacement typically takes between 2 to 4 hours. This includes removing the old unit, installing the new pump, connecting plumbing and electrical, and testing the system. More complex installations, such as those requiring pit modifications or new discharge lines, may take longer.
