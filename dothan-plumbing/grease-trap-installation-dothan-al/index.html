    Hydro-Mechanical Grease Interceptor Installation in Dothan

    Hydro-mechanical grease interceptors, often called HGI units, are compact and designed for indoor use, typically under a sink or on the floor. These units use baffles to slow down wastewater flow, allowing grease to separate and accumulate. BRV Plumbing expertly installs various sizes of HGI units, ensuring proper venting, leveling, and connection to your drainage system in Dothan for maximum efficiency.

    Gravity Grease Interceptor Installation in Dothan, AL

    Gravity grease interceptors, or GGI units, are larger, typically located outdoors, and rely on the natural difference in specific gravity between FOG and water. These are common for high-volume commercial kitchens. Our team handles the complex excavation, concrete work, and plumbing connections required for proper GGI installation, ensuring compliance with all local Alabama ordinances for waste management.
