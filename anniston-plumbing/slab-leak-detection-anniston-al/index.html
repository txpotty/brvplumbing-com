    Acoustic Slab Leak Detection in Anniston

    Our technicians utilize highly sensitive acoustic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a leak under your slab.     Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Anniston, AL

    Thermal imaging cameras are a non-invasive tool we use to detect temperature differentials caused by leaking hot water lines under your slab. As hot water escapes, it creates a warmer spot on the surface, which our cameras can identify. This technique is invaluable for quickly narrowing down the leak area, especially in larger homes across Anniston, AL.

    In Anniston, AL, slab leaks can cause significant damage including foundation cracks, soil erosion beneath the slab, mold and mildew growth in wall cavities or under flooring, damage to flooring materials, and increased humidity levels.     Common signs of a slab leak in an Alabama home include unusually high water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, visible cracks in the foundation, damp or wet spots on carpets or flooring, and a sudden drop in water pressure.
