    Pipe Bursting Sewer Repair in Anniston

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method where a new pipe is pulled through the old one, simultaneously fracturing the old pipe outward. This creates a new, seamless, and often larger diameter sewer line without the need for extensive trenching. It is ideal for severely damaged or collapsed pipes in Anniston that require full replacement rather than just relining.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe (CIPP) Relining in Anniston, AL

    CIPP relining involves inserting a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged sewer pipe. Once in place, the tube is inflated and cured, typically with heat or UV light, creating a new, durable pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for cracks, minor breaks, and root intrusion, providing a smooth, corrosion-resistant interior surface for homes in Anniston, AL.
