    While chemical drain cleaners are available, BRV Plumbing generally advises against them. They often only partially clear clogs, can damage pipes over time, and pose safety risks. Professional rooter services are much more effective, safer for your plumbing, and provide a lasting solution to blockages.

    For most standard drain cleaning or rooter services within your property's existing plumbing system, permits are generally not required in Anniston, AL.     To prevent future clogs, avoid pouring grease down drains, use drain screens in sinks and showers to catch hair and food particles, and dispose of fibrous materials like coffee grounds and wipes in the trash. Consider periodic professional drain maintenance or hydro-jetting, especially for older homes or those with a history of recurring blockages.

    When performed by trained professionals like those at BRV Plumbing, rooter services are safe for your pipes.     Drain snaking, also known as augering, uses a long, flexible cable with a cutting head to physically break through or retrieve blockages like hair or tree roots. Hydro-jetting uses high-pressure water to clean the entire interior circumference of the pipe, effectively removing grease, mineral buildup, and sludge, leaving pipes much cleaner than snaking alone. Hydro-jetting is generally more effective for severe or recurring clogs.
