    Frequently Asked Questions About Leak Detection in Anniston

    The cost of leak detection in Anniston can range from $250 to $800, depending on the complexity and location of the leak. Factors like slab leaks or those requiring specialized equipment may be at the higher end. We provide a detailed estimate after our initial assessment.

    If you suspect a gas leak, immediately evacuate everyone from the property, do not use any electrical devices, and call your gas utility company and BRV Plumbing from a safe location. Gas leaks are extremely dangerous and require immediate professional attention.

    Many homeowner's insurance policies cover the cost of repairing sudden and accidental leaks, and sometimes the cost of leak detection itself, especially if it leads to a covered repair. It's best to check your specific policy or contact your insurance provider for details.

    A slab leak is a leak in the water pipes embedded within or beneath your home's concrete slab foundation. They are common in Alabama due to the expansive clay soils that shift and settle, putting stress on plumbing lines, and the freeze-thaw cycles that can cause pipes to expand and contract.

    Most residential leak detection services take between 1 to 3 hours. The exact time depends on the leak's location, its severity, and the type of plumbing system in your home. Complex slab leaks might take slightly longer to precisely identify.

    Common signs include unexplained increases in your water bill, damp spots on walls or ceilings, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, a musty odor, or visible mold growth. Don't ignore these indicators, as they often point to a hidden leak.

    To prevent future leaks, consider regular plumbing inspections, especially for older homes. Ensure proper water pressure, address any signs of corrosion, and maintain your plumbing system. For homes with known issues, proactive repiping or pipe upgrades can be a long-term solution.

    In Alabama, older homes may have galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes, both known for higher leak rates due to corrosion or material degradation.
