    The duration of sewer repair depends on the issue. Simple clog removals might take an hour or two. More involved repairs, like replacing a section of pipe through excavation, could take a full day. Trenchless repairs might span 1-3 days. We'll give you an accurate timeline during our assessment.

    For sewer line replacements, we primarily use durable, code-compliant materials such as PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride) for its longevity and resistance to corrosion, or HDPE (High-Density Polyethylene) for trenchless applications. We select the best material based on your specific needs and local soil conditions.

    To prevent future sewer issues, avoid flushing anything but toilet paper, don't pour grease down drains, and be mindful of tree roots near your sewer line. Regular sewer camera inspections, especially for older homes, can also help identify potential problems before they become emergencies.

    Key signs you need sewer repair include multiple slow drains or frequent backups, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, foul sewer odors in or around your home, soggy spots in your yard, or an unexplained increase in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's wise to call for an inspection.
