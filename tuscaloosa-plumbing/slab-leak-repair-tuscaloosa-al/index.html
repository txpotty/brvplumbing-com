    Direct Slab Penetration Repair in Tuscaloosa, AL

    When a slab leak is directly accessible and localized, direct slab penetration is often the most efficient repair method.     Pipe Rerouting and Repiping in Tuscaloosa

    For more extensive damage, multiple leaks, or pipes that are too old and corroded to repair reliably, pipe rerouting is a long-term solution. This involves abandoning the leaking line under the slab and installing a new pipe through the walls or attic. This method bypasses the problematic area entirely, providing a fresh, durable plumbing line. It is a common solution for aging homes in Tuscaloosa with original copper or galvanized plumbing.

    Tunneling Slab Leak Repair in Tuscaloosa, AL

Tunneling is a non-invasive interior method that requires skilled excavation and shoring to ensure structural integrity throughout the repair process in Tuscaloosa, AL.
